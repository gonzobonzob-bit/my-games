📻 Radio Station Tycoon · Idle
Callsigns
Sign on with a four-letter callsign and build up to four stations across one city. Program four dayparts on every signal — music, talk, news or paid programming — against a finite audience, which means your second station takes listeners from your first as readily as from a rival. Every station pays a daily lease that scales with the transmitter and antenna you put on it, so reach you cannot sell is just a bigger bill. Extra co-hosts and heavier formats raise a slot's load, load is what breaks transmitters, and one engineer covers one daypart across the whole empire — so the schedule is a coverage problem, not a shopping list. Two or three people come looking for work each week and that is the entire talent supply, forever. Autosaves, with capped catch-up for the broadcast days that passed while you were away. Playable end to end; the economy is still being tuned.
